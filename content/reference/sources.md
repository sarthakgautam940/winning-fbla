## The official references

The 2026-2027 guidelines list these seven references for the event's objectives. Test writers draw their vocabulary and depth from them, so the course covers what each one teaches.

- AI4K12, *Grade Band Progression Charts*: the Five Big Ideas in AI (Perception, Representation and Reasoning, Learning, Natural Interaction, Societal Impact). https://ai4k12.org/gradeband-progression-charts/
- Association for Computing Machinery, *Computing Competencies for Undergraduate Data Science Curricula* (2021). https://www.acm.org/binaries/content/assets/education/curricula-recommendations/dstf_ccdsc2021.pdf
- CodeHS, *Intro to AI Syllabus*. https://codehs.com/uploads/224a0c7cb8de30b494ec9c2286a73a28
- Google Cloud, *What is artificial intelligence (AI)*. https://cloud.google.com/learn/what-is-artificial-intelligence
- Harvard, *Data Science Principles*. https://www.harvardonline.harvard.edu/course/data-science-principles
- IBM, *IBM Data Science Professional Certificate*. https://www.coursera.org/professional-certificates/ibm-data-science/
- Youcubed, *DS Learning Objectives and Standards by Unit*. https://hsdatascience.youcubed.org/resources-standards-and-course-approval/

## Standard texts behind the definitions

Definitions follow the most common usage in the standard sources: Wes McKinney's *Python for Data Analysis*, Stuart Russell and Peter Norvig's *Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach*, Aurélien Géron's *Hands-On Machine Learning*, and the ACM Code of Ethics and Professional Conduct (2018). Where sources differ, the lesson teaches the common definition and names the variant.

## How facts were checked

Every date, historical milestone, documented case, law, and attributed definition in the lessons was checked against a primary source (the original paper, the regulator's own page, the original investigation, or the official documentation) before it was written in. Time-sensitive facts, such as the status of AI regulation, are phrased as of late 2026. Every calculated answer in the question bank is recomputed by code, and every code output shown in a lesson comes from a real run.
